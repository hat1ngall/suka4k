# Product artwork

Source URLs for local assets are recorded in sources.json. Epic Games V-Bucks images contain no denomination; the catalog renders its actual amount separately. Riot assets are from the official VALORANT Asset Kit. Standoff and Apex currency images with printed numbers are used only for matching denominations. Other denominations use game/brand artwork instead.

Logos from Simple Icons are distributed under CC0-1.0: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md . Brand names, publisher artwork and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Other existing platform images retain their sources in ../platforms/LOGO-SOURCES.md.

Images are served locally; product pages do not depend on third-party image servers. No generated currency artwork is used.
